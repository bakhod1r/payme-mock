Payments

Every payment the stand handled, both sides of it: the top-up that took money off a card, the withdrawal that put it back, and the transaction the merchant was driven through. A withdrawal has no merchant transaction — it asks a merchant for nothing — and a call made straight against the Merchant API has no receipt. Both are here, said plainly.
